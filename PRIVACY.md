# Privacy policy

Blank Tube does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and it sends nothing over the network.

It saves two kinds of settings in the browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage`):

- which features are switched on or off
- the video speed you last chose

If browser sync is on, the browser may sync the on/off switches across your own devices. Nobody else can read them.

The extension runs on web pages only to change how they look (hiding parts of YouTube) and to set the playback speed
of videos when you press the speed keys. It does not read or keep page content, browsing history or anything you type.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
